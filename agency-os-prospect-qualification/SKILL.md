# Agency OS Prospect Qualification

Start with the agency's deliverable, minimum viable client economics, service geography, operational capacity, and disqualifiers. Convert these into a versioned ideal-customer profile with mandatory, preferred, and exclusion criteria.

Review prospects from real sourced records. Separate:

- verified company facts;
- estimated attributes;
- observed marketing opportunities;
- unknowns requiring discovery;
- expressed interest or confirmed intent.

Do not describe search appearance or ad activity as buying intent. Score only factors that have evidence, show missing values, and explain why a prospect advances, pauses, or is rejected. Require a human decision before `sales-ready`.

Produce a prioritized list with company, qualification rationale, evidence links, uncertainty, contact route, recommended next step, and disqualifiers. Periodically compare qualified cohorts with replies, meetings, proposals, wins, retention, and profitability; revise criteria from measured outcomes rather than anecdotes.
