# Agency OS Google Ads Oversight

Begin with the client's business goal, service area, economics, capacity, landing path, and agreed conversion definitions. Confirm the account is client-owned and access uses delegated permissions.

Review account structure, targeting, match behavior, negative keywords, search terms, ads/assets, landing relevance, conversion actions, call/form quality, budgets, bidding constraints, location/device/time patterns, and data freshness. Use source-system values with currency, time zone, attribution window, and date range.

Distinguish leading metrics from business outcomes. Clicks and platform conversions are not automatically qualified leads, appointments, sales, or profit. Reconcile downstream CRM outcomes and flag tracking gaps before optimization claims.

Write recommendations as reviewable changes with evidence, expected mechanism, guardrail, measurement plan, and rollback condition. Require explicit authorized approval before publishing ads or materially changing budgets. After changes, compare against a defined baseline or experiment; avoid claiming causality from uncontrolled before/after movement.
