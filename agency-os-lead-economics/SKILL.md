# Agency OS Lead Economics

Use actual dated costs and funnel events where available. State currency, time window, cohort, attribution rule, and whether each input is verified, estimated, or hypothetical.

Calculate at minimum:

- cost per sourced prospect, reachable contact, reply, qualified meeting, proposal, and paying client;
- acquisition cost including provider, sending, labor, media, tooling, and sales costs under the chosen policy;
- stage conversion rates with numerator and denominator;
- monthly recurring revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, churn, retention, and payback period;
- break-even clients and required pipeline for the five-client, $5,000-per-month target.

Keep agency economics separate from each client's campaign economics. For uncertain inputs, provide base/downside/upside scenarios and sensitivity to the few variables that matter most. Do not present projected revenue as realized revenue or platform-attributed conversions as collected cash.

Return a decision-oriented summary: observed performance, confidence, constraint, break-even threshold, and next measurement or operating change.
