# Agency OS Client Success

Translate the signed scope into an onboarding record with goals, measurement definitions, baseline, responsibilities, access requests, approvals, milestones, communication cadence, escalation path, and risks. Prefer delegated account access; never request shared passwords when a provider supports roles.

Maintain a client success plan that connects delivery tasks to verified outcomes. Reports must show date windows, sources, freshness, definitions, spend, leads, qualified outcomes, limitations, and agreed next actions. Separate agency activity from client business results and explain discrepancies honestly.

Track decisions, approvals, blockers, incidents, scope changes, satisfaction signals, renewal dates, payment state, and health risks. Escalate broken tracking, access loss, budget anomalies, lead-quality issues, and capacity constraints early with an owner and recovery plan.

For renewal, compare the agreed success definition with reconciled outcomes, service cost, client profitability, open risk, and future plan. For cancellation or offboarding, revoke access, transfer permitted assets/data, honor retention and deletion obligations, reconcile billing, and preserve the required audit history.
