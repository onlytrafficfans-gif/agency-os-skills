# Buyer Evidence Experiments

Find the least expensive observation that would change the commercial decision. Research and simulated customers cannot validate actual willingness to pay.

## Choose the uncertainty
Read existing product evidence and customer conversations in scope. Identify whether the bottleneck is problem urgency, buyer access, offer clarity, trust, willingness to pay, activation, or retention. Pick one assumption to test. Do not build another landing page if the existing one is adequate and distribution is missing.

For discovery, ask about the buyer's last actual occurrence, consequences, workaround, spending, decision authority, and procurement constraints. Avoid leading questions and hypothetical praise. Keep verbatim quotes distinct from interpretation. Never invent interviews, prospect identities, emails, budgets, or testimonials.

## Design the experiment
Record the target segment, hypothesis, acquisition source, offer, observation, denominator, timebox, effort and money caps, pass/stop/iterate rule, and next action for each outcome. Propose thresholds as operational choices, not statistical proof. A small convenience sample cannot establish market-wide conversion.

Use an existing channel and artifact where possible. Prepare the actual interview guide, offer, demo, or outreach draft locally. Sources must support any prospect qualification; unavailable contact information stays unknown. Public complaints are leads for investigation, not proof those people will buy.

Separate visited, contacted, replied, qualified, offered, checkout started, paid, activated, retained, and refunded. Define each event and observation window. Count unique prospects and exclude internal/test activity. Attribute results cautiously when exposure or source tracking is missing.

Prefer observed payment or a buyer taking a costly concrete action over compliments, clicks, survey enthusiasm, or waitlists. A signed pilot is stronger evidence than interest but still not collected cash. Accept negative results: update the offer or stop the experiment rather than quietly widening the goalposts.

## Close the loop
Save the evidence, result, limitations, decision, and one next action in the project's existing tracking location. No recurring monitoring unless requested. If sending or publishing is awaiting explicit authorization, finish the reviewable draft and identify exactly what is ready; do not report the experiment as run.

Use current official sources for channel terms, payment-provider rules, or pricing when relevant. Do not require extra tools or social API credentials when accessible public evidence and user-provided conversations suffice.
