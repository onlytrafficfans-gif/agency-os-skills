# Agency OS Offer Design

Build the offer around a specific qualified customer, costly problem, measurable outcome, delivery mechanism, and credible proof. Do not promise revenue, ROI, or lead volume without a defensible basis and controllable assumptions.

Define:

- ideal customer and exclusions;
- included deliverables and cadence;
- client responsibilities and required access;
- $5,000 monthly agency fee versus client-funded media budget;
- optional setup/onboarding fee;
- measurement definitions and reporting windows;
- assumptions, exclusions, change control, term, cancellation, and renewal;
- risk reversal that the agency can actually honor.

Test whether the client's plausible gross profit, close rate, capacity, and time-to-value can justify the fee; label scenarios and uncertainty. Create a concise positioning statement, service-package record, discovery questions, objection responses, and proposal-ready scope. Version the offer and connect changes to win/loss and retention data.
