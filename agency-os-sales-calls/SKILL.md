# Agency OS Sales Calls

Prepare from verified CRM facts, the current offer, known opportunity evidence, and open questions. Never invent personalization or imply access to private performance data.

Use the call to understand goals, current acquisition process, economics, capacity, decision process, urgency, constraints, prior attempts, required access, budget, and success definition. Diagnose before prescribing. Confirm important numbers and distinguish the prospect's statements from agency estimates.

End with an explicit outcome: disqualified, nurture, follow-up needed, proposal authorized, or no decision. Record participants, attributed notes, objections, commitments, next owner/date, and consented follow-up in the CRM. Do not mark interest or intent without an attributable statement or action.

For closing, restate the verified problem, agreed scope, fee versus ad budget, assumptions, timeline, responsibilities, risks, and decision path. Avoid artificial pressure or unsupported guarantees. Review conversion by qualification cohort, objection, offer version, and salesperson to improve the process.
